# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

ios

## Users

ServeAI is for beginner through competitive tennis players practicing without a coach present. They use an iPhone courtside to capture one complete serve from a side or rear view, then want a credible, understandable explanation of what to keep and what to change.

## Product Purpose

ServeAI turns a serve video into structured, professional-style coaching feedback. Success means a player can capture or import a useful clip, distinguish video-evidence quality from model validation, understand the limitations, leave with one clear priority and no more than three practical drills, and track change across later sessions.

## Positioning

An honest, privacy-first serve coach that makes biomechanical feedback useful without pretending imperfect phone video is a laboratory measurement.

## Brand Personality

Focused, athletic, credible. The voice is direct and encouraging, using coaching language without hype or false certainty.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI purple, neon cyberpunk styling, decorative glass panels, excessive gradients, cluttered fitness dashboards, social-feed mechanics, and interfaces that present estimates as objective truth.

## Design Principles

- Put the next useful action first: capture, review, understand, practice.
- Make video-evidence quality, model assurance, and missing evidence as visible as scores without presenting any of them as a per-serve correctness probability.
- Use progressive disclosure so detailed biomechanics never overwhelm the coaching priority.
- Keep the camera workflow calm and physical: clear framing, large controls, immediate feedback.
- Earn trust through plain language, on-device processing, and explicit simulated-data labels.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, sufficient light and dark mode contrast, 44-point touch targets, and text or symbols in addition to color for every performance state. Instructions must remain useful without relying on the camera-position illustration alone.
