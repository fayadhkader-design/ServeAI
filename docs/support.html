ServeAI support

Start with one complete serve and a clear view of the player. These fixes solve most recording and analysis problems.

Record a useful serve

  • Record from the side or rear with the phone stationary and approximately 10–15 feet away.
  • Keep the full body, feet, tossing hand, and racket inside the frame.
  • Use good, even lighting and avoid other foreground players crossing the frame.
  • Record at normal speed. Sixty frames per second is helpful when the device supports it.

Select one motion

On Check the Clip, set Start just before the toss and End after the landing. Tap Check Selected Clip so ServeAI exports and evaluates only that range. This is especially useful for downloaded social videos, long recordings, or clips containing more than one serve.

If a clip is rejected

ServeAI can reject a clip when the player is too small, too close to an edge, heavily obscured, poorly lit, or not visible in enough sampled frames. Trim away unrelated footage first. If the warning remains, re-record from farther away with the entire service motion visible.

Understand a report

“Video evidence” describes how clearly the app could see the motion. It is not the probability that a coaching estimate is correct. The public MVP uses on-device body-pose heuristics that have not passed independent coach-validation gates.

ServeAI does not claim ball speed, racket speed, exact three-dimensional rotation, injury risk, or medical conclusions. Racket-drop and pronation feedback may remain unavailable when the necessary arm motion cannot be observed responsibly.

Privacy and deletion

ServeAI does not automatically upload videos or reports. Delete an analysis from History to remove it. When no other report references that clip, ServeAI also removes its private local copy; the original in Photos is unchanged.

Still need help?

Open a public support request and describe what happened, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Do not post a private serve video, participant code, signing credential, contact information, or other sensitive data.
