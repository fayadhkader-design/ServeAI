# ServeAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

ServeAI is a privacy-first tennis serve review app. This policy describes how the public ServeAI MVP handles information.

## Information processed by ServeAI

ServeAI can process tennis serve videos selected through Apple’s Photos picker or recorded with the in-app camera. It also creates analysis results, video thumbnails, body-pose coordinates, recording-quality measurements, and locally entered preferences such as skill level.

## On-device processing and storage

The public MVP performs video and body-pose analysis on the device. ServeAI does not operate a server, create an account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or automatically transmit videos, pose data, or reports to the developer.

Saved reports and ServeAI-owned copies of selected videos are stored in the app’s private local container. Deleting the final report that references a saved clip removes ServeAI’s private copy. Deleting a ServeAI copy does not delete the original video from the Photos library.

## Permissions

- Camera access is used to record a tennis serve.
- Microphone access preserves audio in a recorded video, although ServeAI does not analyze the audio.
- Photos access is used through Apple’s system picker so the user can choose a serve video.

You can change these permissions in iOS Settings.

## Sharing and research exports

ServeAI does not automatically upload data. Certain clearly labeled research or coach-review tools can create files for export. A file leaves the device only when the user deliberately exports or shares it using an iOS destination they choose. Those optional files can contain a pseudonymous participant code, analysis results, pose sequences, technical metadata, and a cryptographic fingerprint of the source video.

Exporting a local file does not by itself grant permission to use it for research or model training. Any separate collection program must provide its own notice, consent process, retention terms, and contact information.

## Children

The public MVP is not directed to children under 13. The current research-data workflow is adults-only and requires a separate, explicit authorization process.

## Security and retention

ServeAI relies on the iOS application sandbox and Apple platform security. Local information remains until the user deletes the related report or removes the app, subject to normal device backup behavior controlled by the user and Apple.

## Changes

If ServeAI’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the new behavior is released.
