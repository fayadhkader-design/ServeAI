# ServeAI Support

## Recording a useful serve

- Record one complete serve from the side or rear.
- Keep the phone stationary and about 10–15 feet away.
- Keep the full body, feet, and racket inside the frame.
- Use good lighting and avoid other people crossing the frame.
- Normal speed is best; 60 FPS is helpful when available.

## If a video is rejected

ServeAI rejects clips when the player is too small, too close to an edge, heavily occluded, poorly lit, or not visible in enough sampled frames. On Check the Clip, first set Start just before the toss and End after landing, then check that selected range. If the warning remains, re-record from farther away with the entire body visible.

## Understanding a report

“Video evidence” describes how clearly the app could see the motion. It is not the probability that a coaching estimate is correct. The public MVP uses on-device body-pose heuristics that have not passed independent coach-validation gates.

ServeAI does not track the ball or racket and cannot responsibly measure ball speed, racket speed, exact three-dimensional rotation, or injury risk.

## Privacy and deletion

ServeAI does not automatically upload videos or reports. Delete an analysis from History to remove it. When no other report references that clip, ServeAI also removes its private local video copy; the original in Photos is unchanged.

## Contact

Open a public support request at https://github.com/fayadhkader-design/ServeAI/issues/new. Describe what happened, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Do not post a private serve video, participant code, signing credential, contact information, or other sensitive data.
