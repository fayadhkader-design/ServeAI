# ServeAI App Store Copy

## Name

ServeAI

## Subtitle

On-device tennis serve review

## Promotional text

Record or import one serve, check whether the video is usable, and receive a focused on-device review with phase estimates, one coaching priority, and practical drills.

## Description

ServeAI helps tennis players review one serve at a time using an iPhone.

Record a serve from the side or rear, or choose a video from Photos. ServeAI checks the clip’s framing and visibility, tracks visible body joints on-device with Apple Vision, and organizes the motion into a clear serve report.

Each report can include:

- An estimated overall serve score
- Video-evidence quality and visible limitations
- Phase-by-phase estimates
- Observable technical measurements
- A focused coaching priority
- Up to three practical drills
- Saved analysis history and progress comparisons

ServeAI is privacy-first. Analysis runs on your device, saved clips and reports remain in the app’s local storage, and the MVP has no account, advertising, analytics, or cloud upload.

Important: ServeAI’s public MVP uses body-pose heuristics, not a coach-validated tennis model. Its feedback is educational, depends on camera angle and visibility, and does not replace instruction from a qualified tennis coach. It does not provide medical advice or laboratory biomechanical measurements. The app does not track the racket or ball and does not claim racket speed, ball speed, exact three-dimensional rotation, or injury risk.

## Review notes

ServeAI has no login and no server dependency. To review the complete flow:

1. Open the app and finish the short onboarding.
2. Tap Analyze a Serve.
3. Choose Side or Rear.
4. Import a single-serve video from Photos or record one with the camera.
5. Confirm the clip and run the on-device analysis.

The app intentionally labels its default output “Vision heuristic · not coach-validated.” “Video evidence” measures pose visibility and capture quality, not prediction correctness. Experimental and evaluation-only model modes are unavailable in the App Store Release configuration.

Camera and microphone access are used only for the in-app recording flow. Photos access uses Apple’s system picker. No data is transmitted by the app.
